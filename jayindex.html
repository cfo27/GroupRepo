Strawberry Banana Smoothie

Recipe by Genevieve Yam at Serious Eats . Used here for academic purposes.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups frozen strawberries (about 10 oz / 290 g)
  • 1 ½ cups whole milk (or dairy‑free milk)
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 tablespoon strawberry jam

Directions

  1. Combine frozen strawberries, milk, banana, and strawberry jam in a blender. Pulse to break up the fruit, then blend on high until completely smooth (about 1 minute). Pour and serve.
